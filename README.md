## The line

> My side brush flicked that crumb under the fridge at full speed — it is the fridge's problem now.

97 characters, one line, inside the 140-character cap.

## Why this line

- It is an excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot, told flat by the
  machine itself — deadpan, unbothered, and already blaming someone else.
- The joke is rooted in the vacuum's own hardware: the side brush, the part
  that actually flicks crumbs around, doing exactly what it was built to do
  and then disowning the result.
- The surprise is a specific, physical detail nobody in the field used: the
  crumb does not stay missing, it gets relocated under the fridge at full
  speed — and the punch reframes the whole excuse as the fridge's problem.
- The punch sits at the end after an em-dash pause: "it is the fridge's
  problem now."

## How it was picked

Eight candidates were written and scored against the brief's five criteria in
order (vacuum excuse, real laugh, vacuum-world detail, clean turn, machine
voice) in three independent rounds with shuffled orders. The side-brush line
scored highest (mean 24.67/25) and won the 1v1 against the dustbin line
("sightseeing tour", 23.33/25) and the Wi-Fi line ("outside the circle",
23.33/25). Field check: 0 hits for side brush, flick, or fridge in all 366
field titles, and no near-duplicate (Jaccard < 0.35 vs every title).
